## Running application

Headless Chromium ran the addon's actual `createPhotoMap` module and bundled MapLibre in the existing browser fixture, with software WebGL. Assets and the placeholder thumbnail were served locally. Vector tiles came directly from the configured default server, `vector.openstreetmap.org/shortbread_v1/{z}/{x}/{y}.mvt`. No production API, database or private photograph was read.

Opened Ueno/Shinobazu Pond at `[139.7715, 35.7131]` and the Sumida waterfront at `[139.7974, 35.7048]`, each at zoom 9, 13, 14 and 16. At every position, viewed light, switched to dark without recreating the map, and pressed the selected placeholder photo pin in both themes. Captured all sixteen views and inspected both contact sheets, plus full-size Ueno zoom-14 light and zoom-16 dark views.

Observed:

- Zoom 9 shows the Tokyo distribution view, sea and rivers, broad green areas and place names. The selected photo remains in front.
- Zoom 13 shows Ueno's green area, ponds, stations and permitted zoo labels; building footprints are absent.
- Zoom 14 adds quiet building footprints. Railway tracks remain gray solid lines. The Sumida river has a different blue from the pond and Tokyo Bay; green areas retain their own colors.
- Zoom 16 keeps buildings flat and shows the finer park paths and surrounding streets. Station, park, zoo and water names are readable; no street, route or address labels were observed.
- Both palettes repaint immediately. All eight theme switches preserve tile-request counts and view-event counts. Each of the sixteen pin presses reports exactly `placeholder-photo`.
- All 58 tile responses returned HTTP 200. No page or console errors and no tile-failure notifications occurred. Recorded request hosts are only `127.0.0.1` and `vector.openstreetmap.org`.

Result: PASS. No functional item remains pending for this visual change. Other household hardware and actual LAN performance were not part of this local-browser check.
